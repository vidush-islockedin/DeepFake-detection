      How it works

      1. Find the faces

        MTCNN locates every face and its eyes, nose and mouth, then crops a square 1.3 times the face size so the blend edges stay in view.

      2. Score each face

        A ViT-B/16 Vision Transformer, pre-trained and then fine-tuned on our Kaggle face datasets, gives the chance that the face is a deepfake. One fake face makes the whole photo fake.

      3. Show the evidence

        Grad-CAM traces the verdict back to the image patches that drove it, and the landmarks split that evidence into eyes, nose, mouth and the rest of the face.
